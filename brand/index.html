05 · Voice & Rules

How Void Speaks

The writing is as disciplined as the design. Three non-negotiable rules.

Show, don’t tell

Never assert significance. Juxtapose concrete facts; let the reader conclude.

Bad: “Tensions are rising significantly.”

Good: “Both recalled their ambassadors within 48 hours. Neither has done that since 1979.”

Arrive late, leave early

Enter at the last moment; exit before the point is spelled out. The gap is where understanding happens.

Good: “A lawyer who’d never been to India drew the border in five weeks. 15 million crossed it.”

No em dashes

Em and en dashes are banned in all on-screen copy. An AI tell. Use two sentences, a comma, a semicolon, or parentheses.

Bad: “Rates cut Tuesday - the third this quarter.”

Good: “Rates cut Tuesday. Third this quarter.”

What the Sigil means

Coverage ring = breadth of sources. Level beam = the bias reading. Foot = the method, fixed. Clear space = one ring height. Never fill the ring; never tilt the foot; never freeze the beam on a bias color.
