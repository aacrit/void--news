Black Consciousness is an attitude of mind, a way of life; (ii) The basic tenet of Black Consciousness is that the black man must reject all value systems that seek to make him a foreigner in the country of his birth and reduce his basic human dignity; (iii) The black man must build up his own value systems, see himself as self-defined and not defined by others;
