Population Registration Act No 30: A Required people to be identified and registered from birth as belonging to one of four distinct racial groups. This Act was more rigid than earlier race classification laws. Commenced: 7 July 1950 Repealed by s 1 of the Population Registration Act Repeal Act No 114 of 1991 1950 Group Areas Act No 41: U Provided for areas to be declared for exclusive use of one particular racial group. It became compulsory for people to live in an area designated for their classification group.
