As far as traditional African values are concerned, the fundamental importance of ubuntu must be highlighted. Ubuntu, generally translated as ‘humaneness’, expresses itself metaphorically in umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu – ‘people are people through other people’. In the words of Constitutional Court Justice Makgoro: “Its spirit emphasises respect for human dignity, marking a shift from confrontation to conciliation.”
