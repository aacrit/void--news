04 13 52 40 CDR (EVA) For those who haven't read the plaque, we'll read the plaque that's on
the front landing gear of this LM. First there's two hemispheres, one
showing each of the two hemispheres of the Earth. Underneath it says
"Here Man from the planet Earth first set foot upon the Moon, July
1969 A.D. We came in peace for all mankind." It has the crew
members' signatures and the signature of the President of the United
States.
