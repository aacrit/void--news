German Bundestag. Printed paper 18/8613. 18th legislative period. 31.05.2016. On the orders of the Young Turk regime of the day, the planned expulsion and destruction of over one million ethnic Armenians began on 24 April 1915 in Ottoman Constantinople. Their fate stands as an example of the history of the mass destructions, the ethnic cleansings, the expulsions, indeed the genocides, by which the 20th century is so terribly marked. The Bundestag regrets the inglorious role of the German Reich, which, as the principal military ally of the Ottoman Empire, despite unambiguous information, from German diplomats and missionaries among others, about the organised expulsion and destruction of the Armenians, did not try to stop these crimes against humanity.
