3 Concerning the Ottoman archives kept in Istanbul, the opening in 2005 of the fonds of the Second Bureau, attached to the Ministry of the Interior, comprising the archives of the Directorate for the settlement of tribes and migrants (studied and published chiefly by Fuat Dündar 7), must be underlined. The study of this fonds has brought to light a number of obscure points of the programme of “demographic engineering” that the Young Turk Central Committee put into effect. It brings decisive knowledge on the administrative planning of the deportations, on the will of the Young Turk leaders to transform the demographic composition of Asia Minor, if need be by eradicating the groups liable to hinder their project.
