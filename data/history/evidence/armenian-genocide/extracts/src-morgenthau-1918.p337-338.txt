"It is no use for you to argue," Talaat answered, "we have already disposed of three quarters of the ...Armenians; there are none at all left in Bitlis, Van, and Erzeroum. The hatred between the Turks and the Armenians is now so intense that we have got to finish with them. If we don't, they will plan their jrevenge."
