In the present Convention, genocide means any of the following acts committed with intent to destroy, in whole or in part, a national, ethnical, racial or religious group, as such : (a) Killing members of the group; (£) Causing serious bodily or mental harm to members of the group; (e) Deliberately inflicting on the group conditions of life calculated to bring about its physical destruction in whole or in part; (rf) Imposing measures intended to prevent births within the group; (e) Forcibly transferring children of the group to another group.
