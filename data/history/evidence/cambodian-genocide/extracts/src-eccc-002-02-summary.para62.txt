62. Finally, the evidence did not rise to the level of proving that KHIEU Samphan actively assisted or facilitated the execution of the genocidal policy against the Cham. The Chamber is also not satisfied that KHIEU Samphan was a superior in the sense of having had the ability to prevent or punish the commission of crimes. The Chamber therefore finds that KHIEU Samphan is not responsible for the crime of genocide by killing members of the Cham ethnic and religious group, neither as aider or abettor nor as superior.
