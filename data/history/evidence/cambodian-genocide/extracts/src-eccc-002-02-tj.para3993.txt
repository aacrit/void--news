3993. Having established that a CPK policy to destroy the Cham population existed from 1977,13253 the Chamber finds that the CPK policy targeting the Cham had as its primary objective their physical destruction as an ethnic and religious group, as such. The Chamber is satisfied that the treatment of the Cham demonstrates the CPK’s objective of establishing an atheistic and homogenous society without class divisions and, in so doing, the Party’s intent to abolish all national, religious, class and cultural differences. The Chamber is further satisfied that this objective was implemented through the CPK’s policy to identify, arrest, isolate and “smash” enemies,13254 and was therefore in furtherance of the common purpose of rapidly implementing socialist revolution through a “great leap forward” in order to, among other things, defend the country against enemies and radically transform the population into an atheistic and homogenous Khmer society.
