And after we arrived into a room we were each photographed. After they took off the blindfold I saw the camera in front of my face already. So then one prisoner would be photographed while the next would sit because we were cuffed together and we take turns to be photographed. And I think it was -- I was then placed in the second floor, which is now marked Building D, and we were put into the long row of shackles. There were 20 or 30 of us in each row of those shackles. And the black clothes that we were wearing, we were stripped of those clothes. Some had shorts and they just wore the shorts. For those who did not have any shorts, they were given shorts about an hour later. They were only like shorts with nothing to tighten the waist because probably they were afraid that maybe we would take the string from the waist tightening to hang ourselves to commit suicide.
