The question is, for the remaining prisoners, how many of them altogether? And when you were asked to go along with them, besides the guns pointing at you, were you cuffed or were a string used to tie all of you up? A. Mr. President, from what I can recall, at that time we left the painting workshop there were woodcrafters, electricians, blacksmiths and there were about 13 of us. We were ordered to leave but we were not tied or handcuffed. We walked with our bare hands but we were ordered to walk only in one straight line. And there were no other prisoners remained in the prison.
