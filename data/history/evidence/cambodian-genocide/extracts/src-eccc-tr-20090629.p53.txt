A. Mr. President, on the day I was arrested I only was arrested alone and my wife and children were at the cooperative number 5. I had two children staying with my wife. The eldest was five and the youngest was six months old. After I was freed and after I returned I learned that my children died. Only my wife survived and she is with me at the present time.
