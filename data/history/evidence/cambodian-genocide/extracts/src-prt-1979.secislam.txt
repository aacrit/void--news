b) Together with Buddhism, Islam was abolished. The Pol Pot - Ieng Sary rigime advocated exterminating muslim believers. During the first months after liberation they tracked down Islamic priests. They killed Hikry Selas, leader, and Hathi Seay Mas Sokri, senior deputy-leader of the Islamic religion in Cambodia. They forced believers to renounce their religion, and to eat pork, at gunpoint. They punished those who refused to eat pork, and in many instances killed them. According to Mr. Nhek Huon, now working in Army Corps I over 1,000 people were killed in Svay Rieng, Kompong Cham province ( document No.2.1.4.01 ).
