The figures presented here give only an order of magnitude of the possible number of radiation-related cancers. The uncertainty associated with these predictions is large. For thyroid cancers, the 95% uncertainty interval (UI) ranges from 3,400 to 72,000; for other cancers it ranges from 11,000 to 59,000. For the number of cancer deaths, the 95% UI ranges from 6,700 to 38,000
