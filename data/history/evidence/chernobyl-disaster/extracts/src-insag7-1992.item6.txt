(6) The weight given in INSAG-1 in 1986 to the Soviet view presented at the Vienna meeting, which laid blame almost entirely on actions of the operating staff, is thereby lessened. Certain actions by operators that were identified in INSAG-1 as violations of rules were in fact not violations. Yet INSAG remains of the opinion that critical actions of the operators were most ill judged.
