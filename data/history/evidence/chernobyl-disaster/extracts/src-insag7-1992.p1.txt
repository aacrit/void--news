The Soviet presentation of the detailed account was accompanied by statements of violations by operators of procedures and rules. In the absence of evidence it was not possible to examine the appropriateness of these procedures and rules, or the protection system, in relation to the reactor's design characteristics. The assertion was made that the accident arose through a low probability coincidence of a number of violations of rules and procedures by the operating staff and those responsible for authorizing the test.
