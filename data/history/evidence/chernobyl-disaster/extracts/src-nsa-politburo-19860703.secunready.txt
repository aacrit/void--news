And what do we have when push came to shove? Chernobyl exploded and nothing was ready: not civil defense, not medical services—they did not even have an adequate supply of dosimeters; the fire services did not know what to do. Everybody shat in their pants. Weddings were celebrated in the vicinity. Kids were playing outdoors. The [emergency] alert system is no good at all. And no emergency shutdown either.
