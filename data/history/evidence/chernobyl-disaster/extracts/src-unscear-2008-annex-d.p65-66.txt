110. The use of theoretical projections is fraught with difficulty. It is extremely difficult to communicate such projections accurately and honestly to officials and the general public. Moreover, there is a limit to the epidemiological knowledge that can be used to attribute conclusively an increased incidence to radiation exposure. Therefore, any radiation risk projections in the low dose area should be considered as extremely uncertain, especially when the projection of numbers of cancer deaths is based on trivial individual exposures to large populations experienced over many years.
