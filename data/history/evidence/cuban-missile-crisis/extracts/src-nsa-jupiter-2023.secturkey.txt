On April 15, dismantling began in Turkey. The day before, as part of the arrangements, the Polaris submarine U.S.S. Sam Houston stopped at Izmir for a multi-day visit that provided positive media coverage. On April 25, 1963, Secretary of Defense McNamara sent a brief note to President Kennedy that the last Jupiter in Turkey “came down yesterday.”
