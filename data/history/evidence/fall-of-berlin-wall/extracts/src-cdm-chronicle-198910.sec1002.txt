2 October 1989 A "Monday demonstration" in Leipzig with up to 20,000 participants. The slogans include: "Freedom, equality, brotherhood," "We’re staying here," "Gorbi, Gorbi," "Allow Neues Forum," and "Freedom for the prisoners."
