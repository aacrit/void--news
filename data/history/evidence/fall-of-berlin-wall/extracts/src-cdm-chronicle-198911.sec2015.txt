9 November 1989 8.15 p.m. According to a progress report by the East Berlin Volkspolizei, altogether 80 East Berliners have gathered at the border crossing points Bornholmer Straße, Invalidenstraße and Heinrich-Heine-Straße. Instruction to the border guards: to put the people off till the next day and send them back.
