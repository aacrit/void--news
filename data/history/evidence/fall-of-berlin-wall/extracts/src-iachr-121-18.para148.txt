Rolando Braddick Vásquez, identified in Case No. 270, was 34-year-old Panamanian national who worked as a seaport engineer.140 According to the statement made, on December 22, 1989, at about 9 a.m. he was in line to answer a call made by Arias Calderón at the “Albrook traffic stop” and “a shootout began”141 in which Rolando Braddick received multiple gunshot injuries. His remains were buried in a mass grave in Jardín de Paz.
