names: Saint-Domingue=Saint-Domingue

The decree having been read, the President asked, for himself and for the secretaries, to take the oath first, which they did at once; then the Assembly took the same oath in the hands of its president. The President having reported to the Assembly that the verification office had been unanimously of the opinion that twelve deputies of Saint-Domingue should be admitted provisionally, the National Assembly decided that the said deputies would be admitted provisionally, for which they expressed their keen gratitude. Accordingly, they took the oath and were admitted to sign the decree.
