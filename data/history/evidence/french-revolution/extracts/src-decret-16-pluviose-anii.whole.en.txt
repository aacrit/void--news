Some debate arises over the wording of the decree. Delacroix proposes one (1). After some discussion of the different wordings, this one is at last definitively adopted amid acclamations and universal applause. "The National Convention declares the slavery of the negroes abolished in all the colonies; in consequence, it decrees that all men, without distinction of colour, domiciled in the colonies, are French citizens, and shall enjoy all the rights assured by the constitution. "Refers to the Committee of Public Safety for a prompt report on the measures to be taken for the execution of the present decree" (2).
