Taken together they make possible the obituary census of the Terror, which we have been able to fix at 16,594 victims, exclusive of persons condemned in absentia (contumace) and a few who were reprieved or who escaped after sentence had been passed (Tables I and III). Further research in local archives might add two or three hundred names; but if every death sentence of the period were known it is extremely doubtful that the total would reach 17,000.
