He was taken to Gonaïves; he was put aboard the Héros: it was there that he addressed to the chef de division Savari, commanding that ship, these memorable words: In overthrowing me, they have cut down at Saint-Domingue only the trunk of the tree of the liberty of the blacks; it will grow back from the roots, because they are deep and many. The captain-general Leclerc, having granted the prisoner the favour of being reunited with his family, gave the Héros the order to sail directly for France as soon as it had received her aboard. Cramped by the cold, gnawed by his regrets, Toussaint-Louverture died at the fort de Joux, after ten months of captivity.
