When I was arrested, I had no other clothes than those I wore. I wrote to my wife to ask her to send me the things I most urgently needed to the Cap, where I hoped I was to be taken. This note was handed to General Leclerc's aide-de-camp with a request to pass it on; but it never reached its destination, and I received nothing. As soon as I was aboard the frigate Créole, sail was set and I was taken four leagues from the Cap, where the ship Héros lay, and I was made to board it. The next day my wife, together with my children who had been arrested with her, arrived there too. Sail was set at once for France.
