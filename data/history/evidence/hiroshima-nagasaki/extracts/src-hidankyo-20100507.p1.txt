In 1945, I was 16 years old. On the morning of August 9, I was riding my bicycle 1.8 kilometers north 
of what was to become the epicenter of the explosion of the atomic bomb.  When the bomb exploded, I 
was burned on my entire back by the intense heat rays of 3,000 to 4,000 degrees Celsius, and also 
exposed to invisible radiation.  The next moment, together with my bike I was blown about 4 meters 
and smashed to the ground by the bomb blast.
