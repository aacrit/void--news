I am not a guinea pig nor am I an exhibit.  But those of you who are here today, please don't turn your 
eyes away from me.  Please look at me again.  I have survived miraculously, but for me, to “live” was 
to “endure the agony.”  The atomic bomb survivors, who reached the maximum number of 380,000 at 
one time, have now decreased to 230,000.  Bearing the cursed scars of the atomic bomb all over our 
bodies, we the Hibakusha continue to live in pain.
