After having breakfast, I was on my way to the university where I was a student, the Hiroshima Technical Institute in Senda-machi. I was in the Fujimi-cho district, about 1.2 kilometers from the hypocenter. I heard a big whooshing sound above me, to the left. I thought it was a bomb and I covered my face with my hands. There was a flash, like a great many camera flashes going off at once.
