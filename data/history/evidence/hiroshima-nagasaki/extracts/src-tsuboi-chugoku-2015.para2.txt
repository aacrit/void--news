As I was bathed in this silver-white light, I heard a tremendous boom. Before I knew it, I was blown several meters through the air. With all the smoke and dust, I couldn’t see. I didn’t know which way I should flee. And I realized that my clothes were tattered and my arm was bleeding badly. Dark red blood was streaming from my waist.
