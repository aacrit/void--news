I heard a first-aid station had been set up there, and I practically crawled to reach it. But I didn’t find one. I thought this would be the end for me. I collapsed to the ground and prepared to die. I took a small rock and scratched “Tsuboi died here” into the dirt. I wanted to leave a mark that this was where I died. It was a farewell message.
