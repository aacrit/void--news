The Committee resolves, therefore, to sanction, for the vindication of India’s inalienable right to free- dom and independence, the starting of a mass struggle on non-violent lines on the widest possible scale, so that the country might utilize all the non- violent strength it has gathered during the last twenty-two years of peaceful struggle.
