We maintain and hold that Muslims and Hindus are two major nations by any definition or test of a nation. We are a nation of hundred million, and what is more, we are a nation with our own distinctive culture and civilization, language and litera- ture, art and architecture, names and nomenclature, sense of value and pro- portion, legal laws and moral codes, customs and calendar, history and tra- ditions, aptitudes and ambitions; in short, we have our own distinctive out- look on life and of life. By all canons of international law we are a nation.
