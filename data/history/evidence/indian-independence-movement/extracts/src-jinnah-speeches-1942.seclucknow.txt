note: presidential address, Lucknow session of the All-India Muslim League, October 1937

Hindi is to be the national langu- age of all India, and that Bande Mataram is to be the national song, and is to be forced upon all. The Congress flag is to be obeyed and revered by all and sundry. On the very threshold of what little power and responsibility is given, the majority community have clearly shown their hand that Hindustan is for the Hindus ; only the Congress masquerades under the name of nationalism, whereas the Hindu Mahasabha does not mince words.
