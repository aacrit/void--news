I still remember the refrain of one of the most popular barrack ballads of that day which .proclaimed most proudly that— "Old soldiers never die; they just fade away." And like the old soldier of that ballad, I now close my military career and just fade away—an old soldier who tried to do his duty as God gave him the light to see that duty. Good-by.
