The KBS Special Live Broadcast “Finding Dispersed Families” was aired daily on channel KBS 1, beginning at 10:15 p.m. on June 30 and ending at 4 a.m. on November 14, 1983, marking a total of 453 hours and 45 minutes of live broadcasting over 138 days.
