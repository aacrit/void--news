The archives constitute documentary heritage of monumental value in world broadcasting history. “Finding Dispersed Families” was an epic television humanitarian campaign. Applicants for reunion totaled 100,952; 53,536 cases were broadcast, resulting in 10,189 reunions. It took 1,641 broadcasting personnel and aired live, daily, for 138 days.
