With regard to the Nanjing Massacre, which has be- come a basic resource for patriotic education in China, Hata [1997], Fu- jiwara Akira [1997], and Kasahara [1997a] have authored representative works. Hara Takeshi 原剛 [2008] examined Japanese soldiers’ diaries, memoirs, and detailed battlefield records concerning the Nanjing Inci- dent, estimating that close to 20,000 people were “illegally murdered.”
