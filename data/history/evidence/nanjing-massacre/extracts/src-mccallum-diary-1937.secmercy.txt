Helpless and unarmed, they have been at the mercy of the soldiers, who have been permitted to roam about at will wherever they pleased. There is no discipline whatever and many. of them are drunk. By day they go into the buildings in our Safety Zone centers, looking for desirable women, then at night they return to get them.
