That the Japanese generals who together attacked Nanjing jointly let their troops loose and, each in his sector, carried out massacre, rape, looting and the destruction of property is a fact already established by many proofs, and cannot be concealed." "The accused and the generals of the joint attack, having led their troops to take our capital, together let them loose to rampage, and those slaughtered reached several hundred thousand. Moreover, the cruelties of cutting open bellies, beheading, gang rape and burning alive were inflicted on unarmed people, simple men and innocent women and children. Utterly vicious, without equal.
