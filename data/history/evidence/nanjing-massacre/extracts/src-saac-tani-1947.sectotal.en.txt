During the period when Tani Hisao's troops were stationed in Nanjing, at the Huashen Temple outside the Zhonghua Gate, at Baota Bridge, at Shiguanyin, at Caoxieshan in Xiaguan and at other places, our captured soldiers and civilians who were shot together by the Japanese army with machine guns, and their bodies burned to destroy the traces, Shan Yaoting and others, numbered more than 190,000. Besides these, of the scattered killings, the bodies collected and buried by charitable organisations numbered more than 150,000. The total number of the victims reached more than 300,000. Corpses lay across the ground; the cruelty was beyond anything in the human world. Its savagery is beyond what brush and paper can bear to describe.
