On 6 February 1947, the Nanjing Military Tribunal for the Trial of War Criminals held a public trial of Tani Hisao. On the afternoon of 10 March, the Nanjing Military Tribunal for the Trial of War Criminals gave its final judgment on the war criminal Tani Hisao. The presiding judge, the lawyer Shi Meiyu, announced the judgment trying Tani Hisao.
