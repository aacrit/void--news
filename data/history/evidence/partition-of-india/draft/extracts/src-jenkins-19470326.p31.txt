3. In the Rawalpindi Division and the Multan District, which have been seriously disturbed during the past three weeks, it was clear that the ordinary law would not suffice. There was, especially in the Rawalpindi Divisions, a concerted attempt by the Muslim villagers to exterminate the non-Muslims or to force them to leave the area by the destruction of their property and by brutal treatment.
