The result was Abell’ s letter to Abbottof 8th August, which you quote. The enclosures were a schedule (I think typed) and a section of a printed map with a line drawn thereon, together showing a Boundary which included in Pakistan.a sharp salient in the Ferozepur District. This salient enclosed the whole of the Ferozepur and Zia Tehsils.
