There is strong evidence to suggest that both Mountbatten and Radcliffe had acted in league with the Congress leadership which was hell-bent on making Pakistan as “small and unattractive”™ as possible. The “popular belief” about Radcliffe awarding “as HE. dictates”’' had turned out to be well-founded. The Boundary Commission Award on the partition of Punjab had been finalized by Radcliffe about a week before the partition; it was then gerrymandered in favour of India and to the detriment of Pakistan.
