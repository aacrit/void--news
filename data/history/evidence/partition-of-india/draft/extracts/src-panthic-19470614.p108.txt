“In the opinion of this conference, the Boundary Commission should be given express directive to make recommendations for the transfer of Hindu and Sikh population: and property from the western part of the Punjab to the eastern part after the partition has been affected on an equitable basis. “This conference apprehends that in the absence of provision of transfer of population and property, the very purpose of partition would be defeated.”
