Today I call to Waris Shah: speak from somewhere within the graves,
and turn today some next page of the book of love.
One daughter of Punjab wept, and you wrote and wrote her laments;
today lakhs of daughters weep, and say to you, Waris Shah:
rise, you who felt for the grieving, rise and look at your own Punjab;
today the riverside groves lie strewn with corpses, and the Chenab is full of blood.
Someone has mixed poison into the five waters,
and those same waters have watered the land.
