Itremains now to examine the statistics of the district of Gurdaspur. It is not disputed
by the Indian National Congress that on the basis of 1941 census the percentage of the |
Muslims is 51,14 and that of the non-Muslims 48.86. The difference, therefore, is not .8 as
was stated by His Excellency the Viceroy in the Press Conference but 2,28 and this obviously
is not negligible:
