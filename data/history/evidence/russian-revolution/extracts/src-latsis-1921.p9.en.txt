In the first half of 1918, the first half-year of the existence of the Extraordinary Commissions, they shot in all 22 people. It would have gone on like this, had it not been for the broad [wave] of conspiracies and the most unbridled white terror on the part of the counter-revolutionary bourgeoisie. To this terror we were forced to answer with red terror and to apply the supreme measure of punishment, shooting, more often. And so in the second half of 1918 more than 6 thousand were already shot, and in all three years 12. 183 [the text layer's reading; the page image of the scan prints 12. 733] people. That is for all of Russia.
