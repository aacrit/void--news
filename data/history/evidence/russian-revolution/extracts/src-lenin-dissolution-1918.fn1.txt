The All-Russia Central Executive Committee met on the night of January 6 (19) and by a majority against two, with five abstentions, adopted the decree. Lenin spoke on the dissolution of the Assembly (see p p. 437-41). The decree was based on Lenin’s draft and was published in Pravda and I vestia on January 7 (20), 1918.
