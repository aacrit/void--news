Art. XIII. — The navigation of the Congo, without excepting any of its
branches or outlets, is, and shall remain, free for the merchant ships
of all nations equally, whether carrying cargo or ballast, for the transport of goods or passengers. It shall be regulated by the provisions
of this Act of Navigation, and by the Rules to be made in pursuance
thereof.
