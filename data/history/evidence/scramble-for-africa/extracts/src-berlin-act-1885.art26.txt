Art. XXVI.* — The navigation of the Niger, without excepting any
of its branches and outlets, is and shall remain entirely free for the
merchant ships of all nations equally, whether with cargo or in ballast,
for the transportation of goods and passengers. It shall be regulated
by the provision of this Act of Navigation, and by the Rules to be made
in pursuance of this Act.
