Art. XXXIV.f — Any Power which henceforth takes possession
of a tract of land on the coasts of the African Continent outside of its
present possessions, or which, being hitherto without such possessions,
shall acquire them, as well as the Power which assumes a Protectorate
there, shall accompany the respective act with a notification thereof,
addressed to the other Signatory Powers of the present Act, in order to
enable them, if need be, to make good any claims of their own.
