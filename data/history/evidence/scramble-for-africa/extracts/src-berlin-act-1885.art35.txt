Art. XXXV. — The Signatory Powers of the present Act recognize
the obligation to insure the establishment of authority in regions
occupied by them on the coasts of the African Continent sufficient to
protect existing rights, and, as the case may be, freedom of trade and
of transit under the conditions agreed upon.
