The critics of the genocide thesis also doubt the truth of the reports on the wretched conditions in the prisoner camps. The term 'concentration camp' is, in this context, said to be used deliberately with defamatory intent. In fact the camps are said to have been set up out of humanitarian considerations, to save the Herero from certain death in the desert. The number of Herero who died in the camps is also said to be put far too high by a number of authors. [84]
The quantitative extent of the destruction is put considerably lower by the opponents of the genocide thesis. Even if it is not fundamentally denied that the number of victims was very high in total, these authors regard the accusation of genocide as unjustified in quantitative terms as well. [85]
