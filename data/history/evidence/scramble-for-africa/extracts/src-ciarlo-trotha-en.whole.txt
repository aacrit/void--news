Osombo-Windhuk, Oct. 2, 1904
Any Herero found within the German frontier, with or without a
gun, with or without cattle, will be shot. I spare neither women nor
children—I drive them back to their people or I will fire upon them.
Such are my words to the Herero people.
The General of the Great and Powerful German Kaiser
This proclamation is to be read to the troops at rollcall, with
the addition that the unit that catches a captain will also receive
the appropriate reward, and that the shooting at women and children
is to be understood as shooting above their heads, so as to force
them to flee. I assume with certainty that this proclamation will
