M. Jules Ferry. Is it possible to deny that it is a piece of good fortune for these unhappy populations of equatorial Africa to fall under the protectorate of the French nation or of the English nation? Is not our first duty, the first rule that France has imposed on itself, that England has made penetrate into the customary law of the European nations and that the Berlin conference has just translated into positive law, into an obligation sanctioned by the signature of all the governments, to combat the trade in negroes, that horrible traffic, and slavery, that infamy. (Lively marks of approval on various benches.)
