4234. The Trial Chamber recalls its findings in chapter 8 that the physical perpetrators in the Count 1 municipalities did not have the intent to destroy a substanial part of the Bosnian-Muslim and Bosnian-Croat protected groups in Bosnian-Herzegovina when they committed the underlying prohibited acts.
