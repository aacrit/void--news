note: the second half of the manifesto and the witness's dating of it, as the trial record prints them; the sentences between this extract and extract seckovner (from 'Oh, despairing people' to 'Everyone there is shot.') are not held, to keep within the cap for a copyrighted record

Hitler aimed at destroying the Jews of Europe. It turned out to be the fate of the Jews of Lithuania to be the first. Let us not go like sheep to the slaughter. It is true that we are weak, lacking protection, but the only reply to a murderer is resistance. Brothers, it is better to die as free fighters than to live at the mercy of killers. Resist, resist, to our last breath.” Please note: The date is 1 January 1942, in the Ghetto of Vilna.
