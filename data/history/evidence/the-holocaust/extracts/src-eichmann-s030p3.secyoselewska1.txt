Attorney General: You live in Ramat Gan, 57 Bialik Street? Witness Yoselewska: Yes. Q. Were you born in the townlet of Powost in the Pinsk district? A. Powost-Zagorodski. Q. In Yiddish it was called Powost? A. Yes. Q. Your father had a leather goods shop there and was considered one of the notables of the townlet? A. Yes. Presiding Judge: How many Jews lived in the townlet? Witness Yoselewska: About five hundred families.
