Q. What was the reason that you hid your identity behind the pseudonym “K. Zetnik,” Mr. Dinur? A. It was not a pen name. I do not regard myself as a writer and a composer of literary material. This is a chronicle of the planet of Auschwitz. I was there for about two years. Time there was not like it is here on earth. Every fraction of a minute there passed on a different scale of time.
