COL. AMEN: Do you know how many persons were liquidated by Einsatz Group D under your direction? OHLENDORF: In the year between June 1941 to June 1942 the Einsatzkommandos reported 90,000 people liquidated. COL. AMEN: Did that include men, women, and children? OHLENDORF: Yes.
