During the Vietnam War,1 the U.S. military conducted Operation Ranch Hand, a program that sprayed an estimated 18-20 million gallons of herbicides—including approximately 11-12 million gallons of Agent Orange2—over about 12,000 square miles of southern Vietnam between 1961 and 1971.3
