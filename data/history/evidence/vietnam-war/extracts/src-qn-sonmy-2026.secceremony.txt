On the morning of March 16, at the Sơn Mỹ Memorial Site, the Quảng Ngãi Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism held an incense-offering ceremony to commemorate the 58th anniversary of the massacre of 504 civilians in Sơn Mỹ (March 16, 1968 – March 16, 2026).
