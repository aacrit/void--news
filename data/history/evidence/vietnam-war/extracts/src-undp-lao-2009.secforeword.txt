Foreword More cluster munitions were used in the Lao PDR than in any other country in the world. Nearly a third of them failed to explode and are still on the ground across the country. These unexploded bombies, as they are called locally, constitute the majority of Unexploded Ordnance (UXO) threat in our country. We in the Lao PDR know all too well the individual, community and national impact of these deadly remnants of war which is why the Government of Lao PDR has participated with enthusiasm in the process to ban cluster munitions.
