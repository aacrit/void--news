PROPOSAL FOR INSERTION IN THE FINAL ACT, SUBMITTED BY THE DELEGATION OF THE STATE OF VIET-NAM » [TRANSLATION3 — TRADUCTION4] The Conference takes note of the declaration of the Government of the State of Viet-Nam to the effect that it undertakes : — To make and to support every effort for the restoration of peace in Viet-Nam ; — Not to use force to oppose the agreed procedure for execution of the cease-fire, despite the objections and reservations it has expressed, in particular in its final statement.
