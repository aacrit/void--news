1958: 73% of Americans trusted the federal government to do the right thing almost always or most of the time – when the National Election Study first asked the question. The 1960s and 1970s: Trust in government began eroding amid the escalation of the Vietnam War. The decline continued in the 1970s with the Watergate scandal and worsening economic struggles. By 1980, only about a quarter of Americans expressed a high level of trust.
