note: John Dean's prepared statement read to the committee, 25 June 1973, on his meeting with the President of 21 March 1973; printed page 998 of Book 3

I began by telling the President that there was a cancer growing on the Presidency and that if the cancer was not removed that the Presi- dent himself would be killed by it. I also told him that it was important that this cancer be removed immediately because it was growing more deadly every day. I then gave him what I told him would be a broad overview of the situation and I would come back and fill in the details and answer any questions he might have about the matter.
