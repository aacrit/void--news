N: Three days later the American ambassador telegraphs Washington that the measures are directed from Constantinople, often in districts where no military operations are likely.
N: One government was the Ottoman Empire's ally, the other neutral, and neither report cites the other.
N: The ally wrote it down. The neutral wrote it down. Neither of them stopped it.

## SCENE 1 | The night of the twenty fourth
# MOOD: rupture
N: The spring of nineteen fifteen. The Ottoman Empire, the Turkish empire whose capital is Constantinople, is fighting the First World War beside Germany, against Russia on its eastern border.
N: Its Armenians are a Christian people, about two million in nineteen fourteen, concentrated in the eastern provinces along that border.
N: This year the government, the Young Turks of the Committee of Union and Progress, will order them from their homes and march them towards the Syrian desert. The record calls it deportation.
N: How many died is a set of counts, from six hundred thousand to one and a half million.
N: Constantinople, the night of the twenty fourth of April. Ottoman police arrest two hundred and thirty five Armenians: writers, clergy, physicians, editors, lawyers.
N: By the end of May the number reaches six hundred. Johannes Lepsius, a German pastor who gathered the consular reports, wrote that eight were released and the rest vanished without trace.

# extract: src-gust-19150617-en.para4
# MOOD: procedure
N: The seventeenth of June. Hans von Wangenheim, the German ambassador, reports that Talat, the interior minister, has told an embassy official what the government, which he calls the Porte, intends.

M: that the Porte is intent on taking advantage of the World War in order to make a clean sweep of internal enemies

N: On the seventh of July he reports the expulsion reaching Trebizond, Sivas and a third province, none threatened by any invasion.

## SCENE 2 | The road
# MOOD: grief
N: Erzurum, near the Russian front, the fifth of August. Scheubner-Richter, the administrator of the German consulate there, writes that the villages hold only women, children and old men.
N: They are driven south towards Mesopotamia and attacked on the road by Kurds and Turkish volunteers. He puts the dead on that road at ten to twenty thousand; the government, he writes, at three to four thousand.

## PERSPECTIVE | The Ottoman government and the Turkish state | victor
# MOOD: procedure
# extract: src-mfa-tr.para7
N: The first account belongs to the state: a pamphlet of nineteen sixteen, a former ambassador's book of nineteen eighteen, and the Turkish foreign ministry's page.
N: The pamphlet, in French, holds that the army commander, finding the Armenians making common cause with the enemy, ordered the Armenian element in localities considered military zones transferred southward, to secure the rear of his troops.
N: Rustem Bey, formerly Ottoman ambassador at Washington, argued that hundreds of thousands of Ottoman Armenians still existed, so there had been no extermination.
N: The Republic of Turkey, founded in nineteen twenty three, holds the line. Armenians in or near the war zone, its foreign ministry writes, were relocated away from the advancing Russian army's supply routes, and greater numbers of Turks died in those years.
N: The ministry, on the plan and on the word.
M: Nevertheless, no authentic evidence exists to support the claim that there was a premeditated plan by the Ottoman Government to kill off Armenians.
M: However, it is factually problematic, morally unsound and legally unfounded to call this episode a “genocide.”

## PERSPECTIVE | The Convention and the parliaments | academic
# MOOD: procedure
# extract: src-un-convention-1948.art2
N: The fifth account belongs to the law. It begins without the word. In May nineteen fifteen Britain, France and Russia, the powers at war with the Ottoman Empire, declared they would hold its ministers responsible for crimes against humanity and civilization.
N: The Treaty of Sevres of nineteen twenty, the peace the Allied powers signed with the Ottoman government, bound Turkey to hand over those responsible for the massacres, and was never ratified.
N: Raphael Lemkin, a Polish lawyer, published the word in nineteen forty four. In nineteen forty eight the United Nations adopted the Genocide Convention, the treaty that defines the crime. One of its acts is this.
M: Deliberately inflicting on the group conditions of life calculated to bring about its physical destruction in whole or in part
N: The European Parliament resolved in nineteen eighty seven that the events constitute genocide within the meaning of the convention, and that no claim against present-day Turkey follows.
N: France adopted a law of recognition in two thousand one. The United States used the word in twenty twenty one, for one and a half million dead.
