# Cinematic variant, 2026-10-03. The fourth Orpheus 3B episode (narrator "tara"; a male
# reader of record for M: lines, a female reader for F: lines), in the Ken Burns
# register: short declarative sentences, documents read plainly, the silence placed by
# the writer. Re-cut from the live script against the evidence ledger
# (data/history/evidence/cambodian-genocide/) and the published thesis
# (data/history/theses/cambodian-genocide.md): every line the document voice reads is
# verbatim from a stored extract, and the extract id follows the marker in a comment.
# Sources are the event YAML, the ledger and the thesis, nothing else. Lines beginning
# with "#" are inert: the parser skips them (pipeline/history/script_format.py::parse_script).
# The mood directive on every segment is the one docs/proposals/HISTORY-AUDIO-ARCHIVAL.md
# defines. There is no clip slot: this episode has no admitted recording, and a candidate
# belongs in the ledger, not here. There is no F: line: no stored extract for this event is
# a woman's words (the thesis says so under what the record omits), so the reader of record
# carries every read. The two survivors heard here are read from the court's transcript,
# which is the court's English; the attribution says "transcript" each time. The live
# script's slogan, its Loung Ung, Haing Ngor, Nhem En, Brzezinski and Vietnamese-government
# quotations and its Pol Pot line are in no extract this programme holds, so none is read.
# Where the record disagrees with itself (the day the regime ended, the size of the prison,
# how many walked out of it, when the court was made) the script states both and chooses
# neither. This file is not the live script: nothing globs this directory.

## OPEN
# MOOD: dread
N: In nineteen seventy nine a tribunal in Phnom Penh charged that about three million people had been killed, under a regime that lasted three years, eight months and twenty days.
N: In two thousand fourteen a second court in the same city put every estimate between six hundred thousand and three million, and the accepted ones between one and a half and two million.
N: Three million charged, two million at most accepted, and a list the prison kept itself.

## SCENE 1 | The road
# MOOD: rupture
N: The seventeenth of April, nineteen seventy five. The Khmer Rouge move at least two million people out of Phnom Penh, with insufficient accommodation, if any.
N: Chum Mey is a mechanic in the city, fixing tractors and cars. He walks out of it asking for Angkar, and is told to move on.

## SCENE 5 | The count
# MOOD: grief
N: Nobody knows how many died. What exists is a set of counts that disagree, each with a name on it.
N: In August nineteen seventy nine the tribunal in Phnom Penh charged about three million, on a survey of one city, one commune, and figures a journalist had gathered in three provinces.
N: In nineteen ninety nine the experts of the United Nations put the scholars at one and a half million and nearly one point seven million, about twenty per cent of the population, and wrote that historians had rejected two to three million.

## SCENE 6 | The word
# MOOD: reckoning
N: The word genocide has been given three answers in this record, by three producers, under two definitions.
N: In nineteen seventy nine the tribunal in Phnom Penh defined the crime by decree to include forced evacuation, forced labour and the abolition of religion, and found it committed against the whole people.
N: In nineteen ninety nine the experts of the United Nations, applying the convention of nineteen forty eight, named the Cham, the Vietnamese and the Buddhist monkhood, and stopped at the Khmer majority.

## TURN | What each one leaves out
# MOOD: reckoning
N: Each is true as far as it goes.
N: The account of the regime never mentions the ethnic Vietnamese inside Cambodia, the registers its own prison kept, or the commandant who said he killed more than twelve thousand three hundred.
N: The account of the survivors is two men who saw the regime from inside its prison; no Cham, no Vietnamese and no base person speaks in this record.
N: The Vietnamese account has two wars in it and no room for the third, the one its own army fought.
N: The account of the court reaches two minorities, and its own judge wrote that nobody explained why.
N: The Cold War account holds the statements of one day and no resolution.
N: And the one count read entry by entry was kept by the men who ran the prison.
