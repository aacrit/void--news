# Cinematic variant, 2026-10-04. The sixth Orpheus 3B episode (narrator "tara"; a male
# reader of record for M: lines, a female reader for F: lines), in the Ken Burns
# register: short declarative sentences, documents read plainly, the silence placed by
# the writer. Re-cut from the live script against the evidence ledger
# (data/history/evidence/haitian-revolution/) and the published thesis
# (data/history/theses/haitian-revolution.md): every line the document voice reads is
# verbatim from a stored extract, and the extract id follows the marker in a comment.
# Sources are the event YAML, the ledger and the thesis, nothing else. Lines beginning
# with "#" are inert: the parser skips them (pipeline/history/script_format.py::parse_script).
# The mood directive on every segment is the one docs/proposals/HISTORY-AUDIO-ARCHIVAL.md
# defines. There is no clip slot: this episode has no admitted recording, and a candidate
# belongs in the ledger, not here. There is no F: line: no stored extract for this event is
# a woman's words (the thesis says so under what the record omits), so the reader of record
# carries every read. Seven reads are Void translations (the Creole broadside, Sonthonax,
# the constitutions of 1801 and 1805, Bonaparte's note, Toussaint's memoir, Lacroix) and
# the narration says so before each; the Lacroix words are attributed, and the narration
# says that too. The live script's Boukman prayer, its C.L.R. James, Napoleon "damn sugar",
# Trouillot and Dubois quotations are in no extract this programme holds, so none is read;
# its "We have dared to be free" is read from the translation the ledger holds, which
# differs from the event record's wording; its "I have avenged America" stands in this
# record in the proclamation of April 1804, not the declaration of 1 January, and is
# narrated with that said. Where the record disagrees with itself (what happened before
# the rising and on which night, the day Toussaint died, how long Haiti paid) the script
# states the accounts and chooses none. Where a figure is known to this programme only
# from the event YAML, the narration says "the event record". This file is not the live
# script: nothing globs this directory.

## OPEN
# MOOD: dread
N: On the eighth of November, eighteen oh one, the First Consul of France told the people of Saint-Domingue that they were all French, all free and all equal. In the same fortnight he wrote to his foreign minister about annihilating the government of the blacks.
N: Five acts said free. One law said nothing. One ordinance set the price.

## TITLE
# MOOD: dread
N: The Haitian Revolution, seventeen ninety one to eighteen oh four.

## SCENE 1 | The night of the twenty second
# MOOD: rupture
N: The northern plain of Saint-Domingue, August seventeen ninety one. Three accounts of how it began are held here, and they do not agree.
N: Thomas Madiou, the Haitian historian, tells of the night of the fourteenth: two hundred delegates of the workshops of the North met at one plantation, heard a supposed decree of the king read out, and fixed the twenty second.
N: The commission of the French Convention reports no ceremony. The fire began towards midnight. By the morning of the twenty third it counted thirty seven proprietors, attorneys and refiners killed.
N: Antoine Dalmas, a colonist, is the one source here for a sacrifice: a black pig, in a wooded ground called the Caiman. He dates the meeting that preceded it, not the sacrifice.

## ASIDE
# MOOD: grief
N: In April Madiou prints another proclamation of Dessalines, announcing the general extermination of the French, which almost every arm, he writes, had carried out. He counts the victims himself, in the thousands.
N: Its text says that its author has saved his country and avenged America. The event record places those words in January. In this record they stand in April.

## TURN | Five accounts
# MOOD: reckoning
N: Five accounts of who freed whom, and what it cost. Each gets its own case first.

## PERSPECTIVE | The enslaved revolutionaries | indigenous
# MOOD: testimony
# extract: src-madiou-1848-t3.p471.en
N: The first account belongs to the people who did it, and its claim is authorship: by the event record they organised through vodou, the markets and the drums, and applied liberty to their own condition before any European abolitionist proposed it.
N: No one of the enslaved of seventeen ninety one speaks here in his or her own words. The case is made by the state they founded.
N: In May eighteen oh five Dessalines, now emperor, gave it a constitution. As Madiou prints it, in a translation made by Void.
M: The people inhabiting the island formerly called St. Domingue agree here to form themselves into a free, sovereign state, independent of all other powers of the universe, under the name of empire of Haiti.
N: Its second article abolishes slavery for ever. Its fourteenth says Haitians will be known only under the generic name of blacks.
N: Women fought at every stage, by the event record, Marie-Jeanne Lamartiniere among them. No source here is a woman's account.

## PERSPECTIVE | The French colonial account | vanquished
# MOOD: procedure
# extract: src-gainot-mace-2003.abstract
N: The second account is French, and in the event record it is about cost: roughly forty per cent of France's foreign trade, over fifty thousand men dead, and a war that yellow fever decided.
N: Bonaparte, to Toussaint Louverture in November eighteen oh one: Leclerc comes with forces suitable to make the sovereignty of the French people respected.
N: On the fever, French scholarship has turned on its own account. Bernard Gainot and Mayeul Mace, in two thousand three.
M: The spread of yellow fever as well as the renewal of the war with Britain are facile explanations concealing the direct responsibilities of the colonial authorities.

## PERSPECTIVE | The Haitian nationalist account | revisionist
# MOOD: reckoning
# extract: src-dubois-garrigus-2006.sec11
N: The third account is the Haitian state's own, from the declaration to the legislature that took on the debt. It holds that the people freed themselves, and that France set the price of recognising it.
N: The declaration Dessalines signed puts the claim as a question, in the Dubois and Garrigus translation.
M: We have dared to be free, let us be thus by ourselves and for ourselves.
M: What people fought for us? What people wanted to gather the fruits of our labor?
N: In eighteen twenty six the Haitian legislature declared the hundred and fifty million francs a national debt, and voted a contribution, payable over ten years, to extinguish what it called a sacred engagement.

## PERSPECTIVE | The Atlantic academic account | academic
# MOOD: reckoning
# extract: src-yakubu-opondo-2025.p166
N: The fourth account is the scholars', and in the event record its subject is silence: Michel-Rolph Trouillot's argument that the revolution was unthinkable to those who watched it, and so went unwritten.
N: Trouillot, James, Fick and Dubois sit in lending collections this programme could not open. What is held is the account's newest edge.
N: Moses Joseph Yakubu and Paul Abiero Opondo, writing from two African universities in twenty twenty five, trace the abolition of slavery to the slave revolts, Saint-Domingue among them.
M: While the former protest was short-lived, the latter soared on to serve as an impetus for the freedom of black slaves across the globe

## PERSPECTIVE | The indemnity | revisionist
# MOOD: procedure
N: The fifth account is a receipt, and this record holds the receipt.
N: Paris, the seventeenth of April, eighteen twenty five. Charles the Tenth signs an ordinance. It opens the ports of the French part of Saint-Domingue to all nations and halves the duties for the French flag.
N: It requires the present inhabitants to pay a hundred and fifty million francs in five equal annual terms to compensate the former colonists, and on those conditions concedes the independence, full and entire, of their government.
N: It does not name the enslaved. It names the colonists.
N: Five acts said free. One law said nothing. One ordinance set the price.
N: How long Haiti paid is disputed here. The one article that dates the indemnity runs it from eighteen twenty five to eighteen eighty three, apart from the later loans. The event record carries the debt to nineteen forty seven.

## TURN | What each one leaves out
# MOOD: reckoning
N: Each is true as far as it goes.
N: The revolutionaries' account has no witness of its own here, and leaves out April eighteen oh four, which Madiou counts and defends.
N: The French account leaves out the note of the thirteenth of November and the law of May eighteen oh two, both the French state's own papers.
N: The Haitian nationalist account leaves out twenty three heads of state in the first century, most removed by force, by the event record.
N: The academic account reads the record in translation, and its standard works are closed to this programme.
N: The indemnity account has the ordinance and no treasury book. What was paid after eighteen twenty six is known here through two French abstracts.
N: And no woman speaks here in her own words. The women appear as an arrested wife, an article of a constitution, and counted victims.
