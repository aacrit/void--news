## SCENE 1 | The pits
# MOOD: procedure
N: The summer of nineteen forty one. Germany, under Hitler's National Socialist regime, has been at war since September nineteen thirty nine and holds Poland, Lithuania and much of the continent.
N: This is the Second World War in Europe. The regime means to kill the Jews of Europe, every one; that, the Nuremberg judgment later said, is what the Final Solution meant.
N: The Einsatzgruppen, SS and police task forces, follow the army into the Soviet Union.
N: At Babi Yar, on the twenty ninth and thirtieth of September, a detachment of Einsatzgruppe C shot thirty three thousand seven hundred and seventy one Jews, by its own reports to Berlin.
N: Otto Ohlendorf commanded Einsatzgruppe D. In January nineteen forty six, before the International Military Tribunal, the Allied court at Nuremberg, he was asked for his count.

## SCENE 4 | The camps
# MOOD: dread
N: Auschwitz, in German-occupied Poland. Rudolf Hoess became its commandant on the first of May, nineteen forty, and by his affidavit was ordered in June nineteen forty one to build extermination facilities there.
N: Five killing centres ran in occupied Poland: Chelmno, Belzec, Sobibor, Treblinka and Auschwitz-Birkenau.
N: By the time Treblinka was dismantled in the autumn of nineteen forty three, its personnel had murdered an estimated nine hundred and twenty five thousand Jews, by the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum's count.

## SCENE 7 | The family camp
# MOOD: grief
N: Auschwitz-Birkenau, nineteen forty four. Roma and Sinti from the Reich were held in the Gypsy family camp, whole families together.
N: In May the SS sealed the compound to kill its inhabitants; the Roma, warned and armed with iron pipes and shovels, refused to come out, and the SS withdrew.
N: On the second of August the SS killed those who remained, almost all in the gas chambers; some twenty one thousand of the Roma and Sinti sent to Auschwitz died there.

M: There is no precise figure for the number of Jews killed in the Holocaust.
N: Its research puts the toll between five and six million: Raul Hilberg's early count of five point one million, Jacob Leschinsky's of five point nine five million.

## PERSPECTIVE | Roma / Forgotten Victims | vanquished
# MOOD: grief
# extract: src-yv-faq.secroma
N: The fifth account is the Roma's, and no Roma voice is held in this record; the memorials speak for it. Yad Vashem states the range.
M: Estimates range from 200,000 to over 500,000 victims.
N: The Washington museum gives at least two hundred and fifty thousand, possibly around five hundred thousand, and says why it cannot be precise: nobody knows how many Roma lived in Europe before the war.
N: West Germany's compensation law acknowledged the persecution as racial only in late nineteen sixty five; its chancellor called it genocide in nineteen eighty two.

## TURN | What each one leaves out
# MOOD: reckoning
N: Each is true as far as it goes. The survivors' account cannot speak for the dead, and says so. The paperwork account dates a meeting to the minute and cannot find the order.
N: The Allied account counts what was known, not what was tried; the bombing of Auschwitz was refused as a diversion of air support of doubtful efficacy.
N: The Israeli account reads a courtroom as a founding, and leaves out the survivors who rebuilt their lives elsewhere. The Roma account rests on a prewar count nobody made, and this record holds no Roma witness in her own words.
