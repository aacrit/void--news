# History data audit, 2026-09-20, part 1 of 3

One of three parallel audits comparing every event's `data/history/events/*.yaml`
against its validated documentary script in `data/history/scripts/`. The scripts
are gate-checked and sourced, so where the two disagree the YAML is the likelier
error, and the page renders the YAML.

Moved here from a session scratchpad, which does not survive the session. The
evidence is the point: several findings in these tables were REFUTED on checking,
and a refutation is only reusable if the evidence behind the original claim
survives alongside it.

Findings acted on, and findings refuted, are recorded in the git history of
`data/history/events/` and in `docs/OPEN-ITEMS.md`.

# Void History Data Audit — Slice 1 (alexanders-conquests .. cyrus-cylinder)

Audited by reading `data/history/events/<slug>.yaml` and `data/history/scripts/<slug>.txt` for all 24 events, then re-verifying every finding below against the actually-served `frontend/public/data/history.json` to confirm it reaches the live page (the export pipeline resolves some fields, e.g. `hero_image_url`, so YAML text isn't always what ships — all findings below were confirmed present, verbatim, in the served JSON).

## Findings

| slug | field | severity | what is wrong | evidence (quote both sides) | suggested fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| bandung-conference | `subtitle` vs `summary` (and vs its own script) | **P0** | Subtitle claims none of the 29 nations had been independent within the prior fifteen years. The event's own summary — and its validated, sourced script — say the opposite: six of the 29 had been independent since before 1945. | Subtitle (served JSON, unchanged): *"Twenty-nine nations sent delegates to a city in Java — they represented 1.5 billion people and **not one of their countries had been independent fifteen years earlier**"*. Summary: *"Of the 29 nations, **six had been independent before 1945**. The rest had achieved sovereignty within the previous decade."* Script (`data/history/scripts/bandung-conference.txt:22`): *"Of the twenty nine delegations, **six had been independent before nineteen forty five**. | creation-of-israel-nakba | `subtitle` vs `summary` (and vs its own script) | **P0** | Subtitle compresses the entire ~14-month Nakba displacement into a single night ("by morning"). The summary and script both place the 700,000–750,000 figure as the cumulative total of the whole 1947–1949 war, not something that had already happened by the morning after the May 14, 1948 declaration. | Subtitle (served JSON): *"Ben-Gurion read the declaration at 4 p.m. **By morning, 700,000 Palestinians were on roads** out of villages that no longer had names."* Summary: *"**During and after the war**, approximately 700,000-750,000 Palestinian Arabs fled or were expelled from their homes."* Script (`creation-of-israel-nakba.txt:42`, under a scene titled "1947 to 1949"): *"**Between seven hundred thousand and seven hundred and fifty thousand** Palestinians had left. | angkor-khmer-empire | `subtitle`/script vs `summary` | **P0** | The city's population is given as two different, non-overlapping numbers within the same file: subtitle and script both say "a million," summary says "750,000 or more." | Subtitle (served JSON): *"...found a city of **a million people** that the jungle had hidden for 600 years"*. Script (`angkor-khmer-empire.txt:3`): *"What it found was **a city of a million people**..."* Summary: *"...had supported **750,000 or more people**. No pre-industrial city on Earth had been this large."* | Since two of three renderings say "a million," the summary's "750,000 or more" is the likely outlier — reconcile to one figure (check the Evans et al. 2013 PNAS source cited elsewhere in the file for the actual number) and use it consistently in subtitle, summary, and script. |
| angkor-khmer-empire | `date_sort` | **P1** | `date_sort` is used by the frontend (`frontend/app/history/data.ts:246`, `dateSort: row.date_sort`) to order events on the History timeline. This event's `date_sort` is 1113 — the year Angkor Wat's construction began — not 802, the empire's own founding year given in `date_display` ("802-1431") and narrated as the opening scene. Every other multi-year event in this slice uses the *start* year for `date_sort` (e.g. `mongol-empire`: display 1206-1368, sort 1206; `the-crusades`: display 1095-1291, sort 1095; `armenian-genocide`: sort 1915; `congo-free-state`: sort 1885). Angkor is the outlier, and it is off by 311 years — enough to place it on the public timeline three centuries later than it belongs, between "The Crusades" (1095) and the Mongol Empire (1206) instead of near the early medieval Southeast Asian founding events. | `date_display: 802-1431` / `date_sort: 1113`, vs. sibling events' consistent start-year convention shown above. | Change `date_sort` to `802` to match the founding date and sibling convention. |
| cuban-revolution | `subtitle`/script (stale figure) | **P1** | Subtitle and script both assert the Cuban government "outlasted ten U.S. presidents." As of the site's current date (2026-09-20), the government installed January 1, 1959 has outlasted the full terms of Eisenhower, Kennedy, Johnson, Nixon, Ford, Carter, Reagan, G.H.W. Bush, Clinton, G.W. Bush, Obama, and Trump's first term, plus Biden's full term — 13 completed presidencies, not 10. The same paragraph also says "Sixty-five years on" (1959+65=2024) directly next to a reference to "By 2025 independent monitors counted...", an internal inconsistency showing the copy was last true around 2024 and was partially, not fully, updated for later years. | Subtitle: *"A failed barracks raid became the government that **outlasted ten U.S. presidents**"*. Script (`cuban-revolution.txt:5`): *"A failed barracks raid became the government that outlasted **ten American presidents**."* Summary: *"By 2025 independent monitors counted more than 1,000 political prisoners on the island. **Sixty-five years on**, the question of whether January 1, 1959 was a liberation or a takeover has never been settled..."* | Update the presidents count (to "thirteen," or reword to avoid a number that goes stale every four years) and reconcile "sixty-five years" with the "By 2025" reference in the same paragraph. |
| congo-free-state | `subtitle` | **P2** | Subtitle's "10 million hands, lives, and futures" reads as if 10 million hands were severed. The file's actual death-toll estimate (Hochschild, 10 million) is a population/mortality figure; no total count of severed hands is given anywhere in the file — hand-severing is documented as a widespread accounting practice, not quantified. ## Notes on things checked and ruled out

- `hero_image_url` is empty in the source YAML for `columbian-exchange`, and several source YAMLs (e.g. `apartheid`, `black-death`) still point at the decommissioned Supabase storage bucket. Neither reaches the page: `pipeline/history/export_history.py`'s `resolved_image()` step re-resolves every hero image to a Wikimedia Commons URL at export time, confirmed by checking `frontend/public/data/history.json` directly (both events serve valid `thumb.wikimedia.org` URLs).
- `key_figures[].died: null` (arab-spring, for living people) and `media[].creation_date: null` (bandung-conference) are handled safely by the frontend (`?? undefined`), so they don't render as blanks or "None."
- Checked `creation-of-israel-nakba`'s two different casualty ranges ("107-254" in the summary, "80-200" in a perspective) — these describe two different massacres (Deir Yassin vs. al-Dawayima), not a contradiction.
- `assassination-of-caesar`'s "23 senators voted with knives" / "23 wounds" reconciles correctly against the named conspirators in the narrative (Casca + Cassius + Decimus + Marcus + "at least nineteen more" = 23).
- Chinese Civil War's `date_sort: 1949` (the end year, not the 1927 start) is a real divergence from the site's usual start-year convention, but it reads as intentional (the founding of the PRC is the article's title event), unlike Angkor's, which has no such justification.

## Summary

Audited all 24 assigned events (both YAML and script for each). 19 are clean. 5 have real findings: **3 P0** (bandung-conference's independence-timeline contradiction, creation-of-israel-nakba's compressed-timeline subtitle, and angkor-khmer-empire's million-vs-750,000 population contradiction — all confirmed live on the served page), **2 P1** (angkor-khmer-empire's `date_sort` mis-ordering the event by roughly three centuries, and cuban-revolution's stale "ten presidents" / "sixty-five years" figures), and **1 P2** (congo-free-state's "10 million hands" phrasing). The single worst finding is **bandung-conference**: its subtitle states the exact opposite of a fact that is stated correctly in both its own summary and its own validated, sourced audio script ("six had been independent before 1945") — a subtitle that isn't just imprecise but factually inverted relative to the rest of its own event page.
